# What would make this a game changer

Not “we rewrote calculus.” The standard course already gets 6 for
x² at 3. The change is *what is allowed to stay hidden*.

## Already in the repo (the wedge)

1. **Isolation is printed.** “With respect to x” is an argument, not
   a vibe. Gauge (seed 2 still 6) is a week-one exercise.
2. **θ instead of Inf.** Dead isolator and `1/0` refuse. Live `0/0`
   channels still rate. The asymptote is not a place.
3. **Product of slopes is named as a different G.** Students see 162
   vs 405 on the same line. The product rule stops being a chant.
4. **Chain is cancellation.** Isolating the output is legal and gives
   the reciprocal. Inverse functions are not a new chapter.
5. **Related / implicit / parametric collapse to one lab.** The extra
   chapters were isolation policy.
6. **fd leftover is an identity.** `fd(x²)−2x=h`. Reconstruction cost
   stays on the page. No “let h approach 0.”
7. **Integral leftover is signed and exact** on listed partitions.
   Midpoint of a linear is exact at n=1. Deg 2 is not. ∞ not invited.
8. **Second derivative is a paid slot or a named stencil.** Quartic
   splits them. No “the” second derivative.
9. **Same pair, two stories.** `(9,6)` is x² seed 1 and 3x seed 2.
   A reading does not remember the situation. That is why jets exist.
10. **Certificates.** A homework number can carry statement, cut,
    discharged/scope, falsifier. The grader fails the cut, not the kid.

## What to build next (this is the list)

11. **Side-by-side problem book** — every standard Calc I exercise
    next to the process cut, same answer when they should agree,
    split labeled when they should not.
12. **Auto-generator of exercises from knobs** — pick V (pair/jet),
    isolate (x / t / output), mix (Leibniz / show-the-wrong-product),
    extract (rate / fd / stencil). Emit a problem + answer + leftover.
13. **Prescreen on student claims** — “f' exists at 0 for |x|”
    refused on this V; “rate(x²,x)=6 at 3” admitted. Teach the gate.
14. **One-page teacher swap** — what to say Monday instead of the
    limit definition, with the 20-minute demo script.
15. **Gauge lab with physical units** — metres vs centimetres changes
    the *number* of the rate. Kills “the derivative of the function.”
16. **History as presentations, not heroes** — Fermat E, Berkeley
    flag, locked t, ε-δ fortress, AD, this cut. Students see packing.
17. **Bridge sheet to the standard exam** — how to write dy/dx so
    they still pass the other course, without putting the ghost back.
18. **Jet + constraint lab** — curvature leftover, why ∇∘∇ is a
    slot, without a manifold course.
19. **Integral as antiderivative check** — rate of a listed telescope
    equals the integrand on the grid. FTC as two extracts agreeing,
    not a theorem about ∞.
20. **Open certificate format** — JSON a homework platform can
    reject. That is how this leaves a PDF and enters a school.

## What would let people wave it off (avoid these)

- Calling it easier. It might not be.
- Claiming it replaces analysis, JAX, or the exam.
- Growing V in silence (sin, sqrt, ℝ) and pretending the kernel did it.
- A manifesto before a problem book.

The game is: a student can *see* the knobs the old course spent three
centuries packing. Once isolation, leftover, and θ are public, the
ghosts have nowhere to sit.
